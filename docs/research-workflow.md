## Corridor research workflow

Each directional corridor has one research record. A → B and B → A are distinct. The record retains the original analytical signals and adds reported departure/intensity changes and newly positive or absent carrier freight within the selected comparison window. These are analytical observations, not verified commercial opportunities or route launch/closure dates.

### Separate, explainable rankings

- **Freight growth / contraction:** order by absolute tonne change, then number of matched months sharing the annual direction, then the smaller period's freight volume. Growth and decline are separate questions.
- **Carrier structure:** select HHI changes of at least 500 points or newly/absent positive-freight carriers; order by smaller period volume, then directional monthly persistence, then absolute tonne change. This is a market-size-first review list, not an HHI significance test.
- **Full eligible universe:** all corridors meeting the original coverage and minimum-volume/departure tests, sorted by absolute tonne change. No editorial size screen.

The first three lists use at least **1,000 metric tonnes in each period**; growth/contraction also require **500 tonnes absolute change**. These are adjustable research conventions through choosing Full eligible universe, not scientific or statistical cutoffs. In the genuine full-year eligible universe, the lower quartile is about 394 tonnes (2024) / 383 tonnes (2025), the median 2,287 / 2,268 tonnes; 1,731 of 2,892 corridors exceed 1,000 tonnes in both periods. The screen removes the smallest reported markets without hiding them from full-universe queries/exports. For partial-year selection the same absolute screen applies to the selected period; it is not annualized.

No weighted composite score is used. Tables expose period sizes, absolute change, monthly persistence, HHI change and the largest carrier contribution. Detail also exposes departure and freight-per-departure changes. Eligibility still requires every selected month in each year and the existing freight/departure thresholds. Reporting presence does not prove an export is complete. Persistence measures direction only, not monthly effect size or statistical confidence.

### Evidence and limits

Carrier filters select corridors and never remove other carriers from HHI denominators. New/absent activity means positive reported freight in one selected year-window and none in the other; it does not establish market entry/exit. Carrier net-change contributions can exceed 100% or be negative when carriers offset each other. Freight per departure is not capacity utilization. No customers, demand shortfalls or revenue estimates are inferred.

Airport names use only unambiguous, country-checked reference matches; otherwise codes remain visible. The original package, analysis formulae, research figures and fixed Shenzhen–Anchorage report are unchanged. The case opens only on explicit navigation and is independent of current filters.

Fifteen corridor rows are shown per page. Detail and case charts are computed/rendered only when selected. Exports are explicitly prepared on request and cover all filtered eligible corridors before editorial screens and pagination. Original rule-level signals remain available as a separate export; they are not duplicate cards in the research feed. CSV downloads use UTF-8 with BOM for spreadsheet compatibility; Markdown and report/chart reads explicitly use UTF-8.
